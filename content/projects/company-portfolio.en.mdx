This project is a **WordPress-based website** created for a local company to showcase their services and work.  
The design focuses on **clean structure**, **responsive layout**, and **minimalist aesthetic** using **Elementor** and custom CSS.

The site works as an **online portfolio**, allowing visitors to easily browse the company’s projects, learn about its values, and get in touch through a dynamic contact form.
